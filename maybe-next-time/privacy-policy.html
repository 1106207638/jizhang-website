This page explains how the current version of Maybe Next Time handles privacy-related information.

中文

1. 我们收集和存储的信息

当前版本的应用主要是本地优先应用。你在应用中主动输入或生成的信息，通常会保存在你的设备本地，例如：

  • 你创建的事项、标题、备注、分类和状态信息
  • 提醒设置、本地偏好设置以及你主动执行的备份或恢复内容
  • 应用在本地生成的统计展示或报告所需的数据

当前版本不要求你注册账号，也不提供云端账号体系或云同步功能。

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

这些信息主要用于：

  • 在应用中展示你的事项列表和状态
  • 生成本地统计、报告和页面内容
  • 在你主动开启提醒功能时，安排本地提醒
  • 支持你主动发起的数据导入、导出、备份和恢复

我们不会基于当前版本将你的信息用于第三方广告画像、跨应用跟踪或个性化广告投放。

3. 权限与设备能力说明

  • 通知权限：当你主动启用提醒功能时，应用可能请求通知权限，用于在你的设备上发送本地提醒。
  • 网络访问：应用运行过程中，某些平台运行时、发布能力或基础连接可能会使用网络访问。当前版本不以此建立用户账号档案，也不声明将本地事项内容上传到我们的服务器长期存储。

如果未来版本新增需要额外收集、共享或远程处理信息的功能，我们会在相应版本启用前更新本隐私政策，并在适用情况下依法取得必要授权。

4. 信息共享与第三方

基于当前版本，我们不会出售你的个人信息，也不会基于广告变现目的向第三方广告平台提供你的个人数据。当前版本也不提供公开社区、多人协作或账号共享能力。

5. 你的选择与权利

  • 你可以自行决定是否在应用中输入相关内容。
  • 你可以自行关闭提醒功能，或在系统设置中关闭通知权限。
  • 由于当前版本主要采用本地存储方式，你可以通过清除应用数据、卸载应用或删除本地备份内容来移除受你控制的数据。

6. 联系我们

如果你对本隐私政策或隐私相关问题有任何疑问，可以通过以下邮箱联系我们：1106207638@qq.com

7. 政策更新

我们可能会根据产品功能变化、法律法规要求或运营需要更新本隐私政策。更新后的版本将发布在本页面，并以新的生效日期为准。

English

1. Information We Store or Process

The current version of the app is primarily a local-first application. Information you actively enter or generate in the app is generally stored on your device, including:

  • Items you create, including titles, notes, categories, and status information
  • Reminder settings, local preferences, and backup or restore content you choose to use
  • Data needed to generate local statistics, reports, and in-app displays

The current version does not require account registration and does not provide a cloud account system or cloud sync feature.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Display your list items and statuses inside the app
  • Generate local statistics, reports, and interface content
  • Schedule local reminders on your device if you choose to enable reminders
  • Support user-initiated import, export, backup, and restore actions

In the current version, we do not use your information for third-party advertising profiles, cross-app tracking, or personalized advertising delivery.

3. Permissions and Device Capabilities

  • Notifications: If you enable reminder features, the app may request notification permission in order to deliver local reminders on your device.
  • Network Access: Some platform runtime, distribution, or basic connectivity functions may use network access during app operation. The current version does not use this to build a user account profile, and it does not state that your local item content is uploaded to our servers for long-term storage.

If future versions introduce features that require additional collection, sharing, or remote processing of information, we will update this Privacy Policy before relying on those features and will obtain any required authorization where applicable.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Based on the current version, we do not sell your personal information and we do not provide your personal data to third-party advertising platforms for advertising monetization. The current version also does not provide public community features, multi-user collaboration, or account sharing functions.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may choose whether to enter information into the app.
  • You may disable reminder features or turn off notification permission in your device settings.
  • Because the current version mainly stores data locally, you may remove data under your control by clearing app data, uninstalling the app, or deleting local backup content.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy-related matters, please contact us at 1106207638@qq.com.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time based on product changes, legal requirements, or operational needs. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
